Methodology

Data sources & limitations

This platform is a policy reference tool, not legal advice. All entries reflect publicly available primary law sources as of the dates shown. Users should verify current status with primary sources before relying on any entry.

Primary Sources

State statutes drawn directly from official state legislature websites, LexisNexis, and Westlaw searches. Recording law entries cross-referenced against the Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press (RCFP) state-by-state guide. Federal statutes drawn from USCA and agency regulation text.

Update Frequency

Entries are verified on an ongoing basis. Each record carries a last_verified date. Records older than 12 months trigger a stale data warning in the interface. Users who identify errors may submit corrections via the survey module.

Scope & Exclusions

Coverage is limited to coercive control civil and criminal statutes, state recording consent law, and selected federal employment and whistleblower protections. Local ordinances, tribal law, and US territory law are not currently covered. Case law citations are selective and not comprehensive.

Limitations

Statutory text does not capture prosecutorial discretion, judicial interpretation, or enforcement patterns. A state with a criminal statute may have low enforcement; a state without a specific statute may have strong common-law protections. Consult a licensed attorney for advice specific to your situation.

Pilot & Sunset Provisions

Some statutes (notably Hawaii HRS § 709-906, a 5-year pilot enacted 2021) contain sunset provisions that require legislative reauthorization. These are flagged with a PILOT indicator and expiry date where known. Status must be independently verified before reliance.

Survey Research Protocol

The Technology & Coercive Control Survey follows Category 2 IRB exemption criteria: anonymous survey research with adults, no sensitive identifiers collected. Data minimization, informed consent, and retention policy are enforced at the platform level. See survey module for full disclosure.
